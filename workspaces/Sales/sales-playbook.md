# Synthetic Sales Playbook

## Qualification

Confirm the workplace problem, affected users, expected outcome, data boundary,
approval requirements, and definition of success before proposing a workflow.

## Discovery prompts

1. Which repetitive task currently consumes the most time?
2. Which files or systems contain the necessary source information?
3. What actions require explicit human approval?
4. What result would be useful enough to adopt every week?

## Opportunity hygiene

Every synthetic opportunity should have a dated next step, a realistic stage,
and a documented risk. Probability is a planning estimate, not a commitment.

## Restrictions

This workspace does not authorize external communication, CRM writes, contract
changes, pricing commitments, or access to a different workspace.
