# Expense Policy

> Synthetic test policy.

## Principles

Business expenses must be necessary, reasonable, documented, and assigned to
the correct cost center. A receipt is required for any individual expense of
USD 25 or more.

## Approval thresholds

| Expense | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 250 | Cost-center owner |
| USD 251–1,500 | Department lead |
| Above USD 1,500 | Finance Operations |

## Timing

Expense reports should be submitted within 15 calendar days. Finance may
return incomplete reports for correction. This document does not authorize
payments or changes to financial systems.
