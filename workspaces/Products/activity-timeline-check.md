# Activity Timeline Check

## Current product priorities

1. **Codex in Lark controlled workspaces** — Complete safe read and approved-write workflows. This Q3 initiative is in progress with high confidence.
2. **Research brief templates** — Produce consistent, evidence-backed briefs. This Q3 initiative is in discovery with medium confidence.
3. **Meeting action extraction** — Convert meeting notes into reviewable execution plans. This Q3 initiative is planned with medium confidence.

## Important customer risks

1. **Unclear workspace context:** Operations feedback indicates that workspace names do not make the active local folder clear. This creates a trust and execution risk for controlled-workspace tasks.
2. **Poor artifact discoverability:** Leadership feedback indicates that completed reports are difficult to find and download. This could reduce the practical value and adoption of reporting workflows.

## Release-metrics snapshot

Across four releases from June 18 to August 12, 2026, change failure rate improved from 4.2% to 2.4%, median task time fell from 6.8 to 5.5 minutes, and user satisfaction increased from 4.1 to 4.5. The latest release, **Live activity timeline**, delivered the best result on all three measures. Median task time briefly increased from 5.9 to 6.1 minutes with **Dynamic approvals** before dropping to 5.5 minutes in the latest release.
